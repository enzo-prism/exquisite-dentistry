# Finding the Best Cosmetic Dentist in the USA & the World

Medical professionals do not like to rank one another, and “the best” is a hard claim to prove. What you can look for is clearer: a calm visit, careful planning, and results that look like your own teeth. Your smile deserves that kind of attention, whether you are searching in Los Angeles or comparing dentists from farther away.

Dr. Alexie Aguil, a cosmetic dentist in Los Angeles, describes a few of the services and details worth asking about.

### Consider the environment

Many offices keep to the medical essentials, and there is nothing wrong with that. Bright lights and a busy waiting room are still common. A different setting can make a long visit easier. At Exquisite Dentistry, patients are greeted at the door, offered water or tea, and seated in a private room. Lighting stays soft. You can listen to music or Netflix through noise-canceling headphones while treatment is underway.

### Ask what they can actually do

A useful cosmetic practice should be able to explain, in plain language, when each option fits:

- Aligners such as Invisalign, including whether a faster plan is appropriate for your case
- Crowns and bridges that match the teeth next to them
- Porcelain veneers for chips, discoloration, or shape, usually over a few visits
- Imaging that helps you see the plan before anything is bonded

Lower-grade restorations can look bulky or dull. The consultation is the place to ask how shade, shape, and bite will be handled.

### Ask about the technology

On a first visit, ask what they use to plan the work. High-resolution intraoral photos and 3D imaging (including cone beam scans when implants are part of the plan) help you see the current teeth and a preview of the proposed result. The tool matters less than whether the dentist will walk you through the images and the trade-offs.
