# Top 4 Netflix Shows to Explore From the Dentist’s Chair

Dental visits feel shorter when you have something calm to watch. At Exquisite Dentistry on Wilshire Blvd, patients can stream Netflix through noise-canceling headphones and ceiling screens while we work. The setting is quiet, the chair is still, and a visual series is often easier than a plot-heavy drama.

These four titles were still available on Netflix in the United States as of August 2026. We chose them because they are easy to follow in short stretches and do not ask you to keep track of a complicated story.

## 1. Our Planet

[Our Planet](https://www.netflix.com/title/80049832) and its follow-up, Our Planet II, are nature documentaries narrated by David Attenborough. The camera stays on landscapes, wildlife, and slow aerial shots. That pace works well in the chair: you can look up, look back, and still know where you are.

If you prefer a similar series, [Life on Our Planet](https://www.netflix.com/title/80213846) is also on Netflix and follows the same visual, low-dialogue style.

## 2. The Great British Baking Show

[The Great British Baking Show](https://www.netflix.com/title/80063224) is a gentle competition. Episodes have a clear beginning and end, the voices stay even, and there is no rush to the next scene. It is one of the easier series to pause if we need a moment with you, then pick up again.

Holiday and junior editions sit in the same Netflix collection if you want a shorter episode.

## 3. Somebody Feed Phil

[Somebody Feed Phil](https://www.netflix.com/title/80146601) follows Phil Rosenthal through meals and neighborhoods. The tone is warm rather than frantic, and most of the pleasure is in the food and the people on screen. It gives you something to look at without asking you to follow a mystery.

## 4. Chef’s Table

[Chef’s Table](https://www.netflix.com/title/80007945) is built around kitchens, plating, and quiet interviews. The cinematography is the point. You do not need to remember last week’s episode. That makes it a steady choice for longer visits such as veneers, implants, or Invisalign checks.

## What the visit feels like

Streaming is one part of a quieter visit. Patients also have ceiling screens, noise-canceling headphones, and the option to sit with music or silence. The rooms are private. Titles rotate on Netflix. If one of these four has left the service when you visit, we can help you find another calm option, or you can bring a show you already know.
