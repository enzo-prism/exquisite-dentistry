# GA4 audit — August 23, 2026

## Verdict

The prior GA4 implementation was not reliable enough for marketing decisions. Its reported leads were contact-page visits, it missed React route changes, and local/test traffic reached production properties. The source changes described in `ga4-measurement-plan.md` correct the website-side measurement defects. The Analytics and Ads Admin actions below still have to be completed before conversion reporting is trusted.

## Acquisition baseline

The canonical property attributed 794 of 970 sessions to Direct, about 82%. Organic Search supplied 158 sessions; Organic Social and Referral supplied six each; Unassigned supplied four; AI assistants supplied two. The unusually high Direct share is consistent with the prior delayed campaign capture and means this baseline should not be used to judge channel efficiency.

Top landing pages included:

| Landing page | Sessions | Reported key events |

The 40 sessions and 40 key events on `/contact/` are further evidence of the false lead rule. Treat historical conversion rate, channel lead counts, and Ads optimization based on these events as invalid.

## Website defects confirmed before the fix

- A cold landing emitted a page view, but React navigation from `/` to `/about/` emitted no additional GA page view.
- A visitor landing with paid campaign parameters and navigating before the deferred tag loaded lost the paid landing URL; GA used the later route as the page location.
- Local development made real requests to both production GA destinations and Hotjar.
- Schedule, contact, and other CTA clicks were mislabeled as a submitted lead and used unverified Ads conversion labels.
- Several payloads used nested `custom_parameters`, full URLs, timestamps, exact financing amounts, video IDs, or other high-cardinality values.

## Required Admin actions

1. Remove the custom `generate_lead` rule based on visiting `/contact`.
2. Mark only the website's confirmed-success `generate_lead` event as a key event.
3. Disable Enhanced Measurement history-change page views before the manual SPA page views are released.
4. Confirm ownership of source destination `G-RECCC1K9GK` and the linked routing into property `478101252`.
5. Link the canonical GA4 property to the correct Ads account, verify auto-tagging, and import the real `generate_lead` once.
6. Keep schedule, call, financing, and CTA clicks secondary. Do not use them as primary lead conversions.
7. Establish a privacy-reviewed scheduler or CRM handoff before optimizing to booked or qualified appointments. The current cross-origin scheduler exposes clicks, not confirmed bookings.

## Reporting reset

Use the production release date as a measurement reset. Validate Realtime and DebugView first, annotate the release in reporting, and compare channel quality only after enough post-release traffic accumulates. Do not blend the old false-lead period into the new conversion-rate baseline.
