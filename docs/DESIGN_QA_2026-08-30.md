# Design QA, August 30, 2026

## Scope

Production audit of exquisitedentistryla.com against the current `main` codebase. This pass focused on visual/layout defects, not the insurance-copy items still waiting on practice sign-off.

## Bugs found and fixed

### 1. Gold buttons failed WCAG AA contrast

Brand gold is `hsl(38 24% 37%)`, a dark bronze. Black text on that surface is about 3.2:1. White text is about 6.5:1.

The button system still forced black text (`button.tsx` + `src/index.css`), so homepage booking CTAs, insurance-band buttons, and other gold fills were unreadable. Navbar and the homepage hero had local `!text-white` workarounds; the rest of the site did not.

**Fix:** Gold/primary/secondary fills and gold-fill hover states now use white text. Outline hover fills use white text too. CSS selectors match whole class tokens (`[class~="bg-primary"]`) so `hover:bg-primary` on outline buttons is not treated as a filled gold button.

### 2. Cherry widget vanished on Safari mid-scroll

Client P0: the floating Cherry pill disappeared while scrolling down in Safari and only came back on scroll-up. Chromium was fine.

The launcher hid whenever `window.scrollY < 96`. iOS/macOS Safari toolbar collapse and rubber-banding can drop `scrollY` while the visitor is still mid-page, which matches that report. The mount also used `translate3d(0,0,0)`, which can reintroduce containing-block issues in Safari.

**Fix:** Measure scroll with `visualViewport` (pageTop / offsetTop) plus layout scroll. Reveal after 96px, hide only at the true top (≤16px), and ignore the jitter band in between. Keep the mount `position: fixed` with `transform: none`. Listen to `visualViewport` scroll/resize as well as window scroll.

### 3. Broken social preview images

Invisalign and implant routes pointed `og:image` at `lovable-uploads/dc09fcc1-…webp`, which has never existed. Slack/iMessage/Facebook previews 404'd.

**Fix:** Those routes now use `DEFAULT_OG_IMAGE` (`dr-aguil-banner-2024-m.webp`).

### 4. Blog posts overflowed and nested typography on small screens

Every published article used the same shell, and that shell had three layout bugs:

- Imported HTML was wrapped in `.prose` inside another `.prose`, so Tailwind Typography compounded spacing.
- The article column added `px-4` twice, squeezing 320px phones.
- FAQ `<summary>` rows were flex items that would not wrap next to the `+` icon.
- Component posts (before/after gallery, single-tooth) used the old `container` + 2rem padding and stacked three large CTAs from the `sm` breakpoint.
- `main` was a flex child without `min-w-0`, so wide descendants could force horizontal scroll.

**Fix:** Unwrap the inner prose shell at sanitize time (and stop generating it). One padded column, wrapping FAQ rows, `min-w-0` on the app shell, full-bleed before/after bands, and a Playwright sweep of all 66 published posts at 320 / 390 / 768 / 1280.

A second pass then tightened tablet and phone composition that still looked cramped after overflow was gone:

- Three large CTAs and in-article booking links now wrap and stay full-width below `lg`, instead of `whitespace-nowrap` pushing past 320px.
- Before/after proof cards wait until `lg` for three columns; gallery section headers stack above their buttons through tablet.
- Related resources stay one column until `lg`, and comparison-slider Before/After badges shrink on narrow cards.
- Lists use outside bullets so wrapped lines stay aligned.

## What was already healthy

- Hero poster-first behavior and the shared black-and-gold placeholder.
- Footer compact/accordion layout below `lg`.
- Cherry copy remaining fully visible at 320px, with Concierge and homepage FAB collision rules.
- Insurance and payment bands, team page, and the main service templates.

## Visual audit notes (not bugs)

A live-site pass on `/`, `/veneers/`, and `/blog/` flagged the Cherry “Pay over time” pill covering nearby headings on 390px. That overlay is the intended conversion control, not a layout defect.

Collision rules stay the same: do not shrink the launcher to icon-only, keep full copy at every width, and keep it from overlapping the Concierge or homepage FAB. Overlays (nav, search, dialogs, consent) stay above the pill. Body copy can sit under the fixed launcher while the visitor scrolls.

Insurance wording P0s remain blocked on Michael’s copy approval and were out of scope for this pass.
