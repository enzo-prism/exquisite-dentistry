## Policy and review notes

- The set does not depict patients or imply anyone's health status, dissatisfaction, treatment result, or testimonial.
- Keep ad copy neutral and service-led. Avoid guaranteed outcomes, appearance pressure, personal-attribute language, and unsupported credential or superlative claims.
- Confirm Dr. Aguil's paid-media image rights and the logo/background asset provenance before launch.
- The monochrome source was natively 1024 × 1024. Its 1200 × 1200 export is a modest upscale for consistent platform handling; use the office portrait or brand control if the upload preview appears soft.
- Review the final platform crop and compression preview before publishing.
